Overview
A land of extraordinary contrasts

Assam is globally renowned for its sweeping tea gardens, rich wildlife, and vibrant tribal culture. The mighty Brahmaputra River flows through its heart, creating vast plains and river islands. Dispur serves as the administrative capital (within Guwahati), while Dima Hasao — Assam's own "Switzerland of the Northeast" — enchants with rolling green hills and misty lakes.

🌊

The Brahmaputra

The mighty river defines Assam's geography, carving vast plains and nurturing unique biodiversity.

🏔️

Southern Hills

Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao hills frame the south, home to Haflong — Assam's only hill station.

🦏

Wildlife Corridor

Central districts host Kaziranga and Pobitora — home to the world's largest one-horned rhino population.

🏛️

Ahom Heartland

Sivasagar and Jorhat in the east preserve the rich legacy of the ancient Ahom Kingdom.

Attractions
What to see & explore
Sivasagar

Rang Ghar

Believed to be Asia's oldest and largest surviving amphitheater — a royal arena from the Ahom era.

Sivasagar

Talatal Ghar

The ancient underground palace of the Ahom Kings with secret passages and multiple floors.

Sivasagar

Charaideo Pyramids

Unique burial pyramids of Ahom royalty — Assam's answer to the great monuments of antiquity.

Majuli Island

Samaguri Satra

Watch artisans craft intricate traditional masks used in Vaishnavite religious theater performances.

Guwahati

Mayong Village Museum

Relics, ancient swords, and the fascinating history of Assam's mysterious black magic practices.

Sivasagar

Joysagar Tank

The largest man-made water tank in the world, built by the Ahom King Sivasingha in 1734.

UNESCO Heritage

Kaziranga National Park

Home to two-thirds of the world's one-horned rhinos, plus Bengal tigers, elephants, and water buffaloes.

Wildlife Sanctuary

Pobitora

The highest density of one-horned rhinos anywhere in the world. Excellent jeep and elephant safaris.

Dima Hasao

Jatinga Valley

A mysterious valley where migratory birds inexplicably crash on foggy September–November nights.

Dima Hasao

Panimoor Waterfalls

Majestic cascading waters tucked inside the misty hills of Dima Hasao district.

Haflong

Haflong Lake

A serene, tranquil lake and the scenic Moulpong View Point in Assam's only hill station.

Majuli

Majuli River Island

The world's largest river island — serene village life, stilt houses, and Brahmaputra sunsets.

Guwahati

Kamakhya Temple

A 16th-century Shakti Peeth with a unique sanctum containing no idol — only a sacred Yoni Kund. Get a VIP ticket (₹501) to skip the 7-8 hour queue.

Majuli Island

Satras (Monasteries)

Centers of Neo-Vaishnavite philosophy, music, and dance. The spiritual soul of Assamese culture.

Sivasagar

Sivadol

A Shiva temple with a Lingam in a hollow depression, flanked by Vishnu Dol and Devi Dol.

Nagaon

Maha Mrityunjay Temple

Features a towering 126-foot-tall Shiva Linga — one of the tallest in India.

Cuisine
Food & local flavours

Assamese cuisine is defined by mild spices, organic ingredients, and the staple of rice. The traditional Assamese Thali is complemented by distinctive tribal cuisines across the state.

Assamese Thali

Mild, organic, rice-based with Khar (fish & papaya) and the sour kick of Ou Tenga (elephant apple).

Missing Thali

Boiled greens, steamed fish, roasted tomato and brinjal, local chicken, and traditional namsing.

Kuki & Dimasa Tribal

Organic vegetables, boiled mustard leaves (Lai Patta), and chicken with rice — minimal spices.

Tribal Beverages

Apong (Missing rice beer with local herbs) and Judima (Dimasa sticky rice beer).

Festivals
Cultural experiences
1

Ambubachi Mela — June

Held at Kamakhya Temple; a rare festival celebrating the annual menstruation of the Goddess — one of the most unique religious gatherings in India.

2

Raas Leela — November

A vibrant celebration of Lord Krishna, particularly spectacular at the Majuli Satras.

3

Durga Puja / Navratri

Celebrated with exceptional fervor at the Kamakhya Temple and across the state.

4

Tea Tasting Year-Round

Visit the auction centers in Guwahati or tour the lush estates of Jorhat and Dibrugarh.

When to Visit
Best time of year
May Jun Jul Aug Sep Oct Nov Dec Jan Feb Mar Apr

November–April is peak season with clear skies. Monsoon (May–Oct) brings floods and safari closures. Visit Sep–Nov for the mysterious Jatinga bird phenomenon.

Practical Tips
Know before you go
🛕

Kamakhya Temple

General queues take 7–8 hours. Buy a VIP ticket (₹501) to reduce wait to 2–3 hours.

🦏

Safaris

Book Kaziranga and Pobitora safaris in advance. Morning safaris offer the best wildlife sightings.

⛵

Majuli Ferries

Arrive at Nimati Ghat well before 5:00 PM — no ferries operate after that time.

🦅

Jatinga Birds

The bird phenomenon occurs on dark, foggy, windless nights between 7 PM and 10 PM, Aug–Nov.

Sample Itinerary
5 days in Assam — a first-timer's journey

From sacred temples in Guwahati to river island sunsets in Majuli — a curated route that covers the very best of Assam.

Day 1
Day 1 Guwahati & Pobitora
Morning
Kamakhya Temple — Begin with blessings at Guwahati's iconic Shakti Peeth on Nilachal Hill. Buy VIP ticket ₹501
Afternoon
Drive 50 km to Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary for a jeep safari among the world's densest one-horned rhino population. Stop at Mayong Village Museum on the way back.
Evening
Return to Guwahati. Ride the scenic Brahmaputra Ropeway at sunset, then explore street food and shopping at Fancy Bazaar.
Day 2
Day 2 Dima Hasao & Haflong
Morning
Board the Vistadome train from Guwahati to New Haflong. Panoramic glass windows reveal lush hills and the spectacular Dayang Railway Bridge. Runs twice weekly
Afternoon
Check into a Dimasa tribal homestay. Enjoy a traditional lunch paired with Judima sticky rice beer. Visit serene Haflong Lake and Moulpong View Point.
Evening
Head to the Jatinga Bird Watchtower. Between Aug–Nov, witness the mysterious phenomenon of birds flying into lights on foggy nights. 7 PM – 10 PM
Day 3
Day 3 Kaziranga National Park
Morning
Depart Haflong early for Kaziranga (~5–6 hrs). Stop en route at the spectacular Panimoor Waterfalls for a break.
Afternoon
Arrive in Kaziranga, check into a forest resort. Relax over an Assamese Thali lunch. Explore the resort grounds.
Evening
Western Zone Jeep Safari — prime time to spot one-horned rhinos, wild water buffaloes, and perhaps a Bengal tiger. Book in advance
Day 4
Day 4 Sivasagar & Jorhat
Morning
Drive to Sivasagar (~2 hrs) to explore the Ahom Kingdom — visit the Charaideo Pyramids, Sivadol, Talatal Ghar palace, and Rang Ghar amphitheater.
Afternoon
Travel to Jorhat, the Tea Capital. Take a guided walk through sprawling tea estates, observing tea leaves and black pepper vines being harvested.
Evening
Settle into Jorhat. Try traditional dishes with Ou Tenga (elephant apple) and Khar at a local restaurant.
Day 5
Day 5 Majuli Island
Morning
Catch the morning ferry from Nimati Ghat to Majuli (~1.5 hrs). Rent a bicycle and head to Samaguri Satra to watch traditional mask-making artisans at work.
Afternoon
Tour a Missing tribe village — observe bamboo stilt houses built for monsoon floods. Enjoy an authentic outdoor Missing Thali with homemade Apong rice beer.
Evening
Watch the sunset over the Brahmaputra from the island banks. Catch the last ferry back to the mainland. Must depart before 5 PM

Trip summary

This 5-day route takes you from the spiritual energy of Guwahati → the misty hills of Haflong → the wild grasslands of Kaziranga → the ancient Ahom ruins of Sivasagar → to the serene river island of Majuli. Best done between November and April to avoid monsoon closures. Ideal for first-time visitors wanting the full spectrum of Assam's magic.
